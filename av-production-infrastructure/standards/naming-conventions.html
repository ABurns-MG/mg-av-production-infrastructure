Naming Conventions

Purpose

Consistent naming prevents confusion across systems, rooms, and documentation.

Format

[LOCATION]-[SYSTEM]-[TYPE]-[IDENTIFIER]

Examples

NameMeaning
MS-AUD-DANTE-CH61Main Sanctuary, Audio, Dante Channel 61
MS-VID-CAM1Main Sanctuary, Video, Camera 1
EVT-VID-CAM2Event Center, Video, Camera 2

Location Codes

  • MS – Main Sanctuary
  • KM – Kids Ministry
  • YTH – Youth
  • EVT – Event Center

System Codes

  • AUD – Audio
  • VID – Video
  • LTG – Lighting
  • BRD – Broadcast
  • IMG – IMAG
